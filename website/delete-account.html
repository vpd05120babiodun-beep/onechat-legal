OneChat

Delete your account

You can delete your OneChat account and its data at any time, either inside the app or by emailing us.

Delete it in the app

  1. Open OneChat and go to the Settings tab.
  2. Tap Security & Privacy, then Delete account.
  3. Type your password to confirm, then tap Delete my account.

Deletion starts straight away and cannot be undone.

Cannot use the app? Ask us by email

Send an email to onechat.support@gmail.com with the subject Delete my OneChat account. Tell us the email address and username you signed up with. We may ask you to confirm that the account is yours. We will delete the account and reply within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your profile, username, phone number and profile photo.
  • Your messages, photos, documents and voice notes.
  • Your statuses and connection requests.
  • Your messages in Rooms and your place in each room.
  • Your place in groups. Your name in groups is replaced with "Deleted account".
  • Your sign-in and notification settings.

What may remain for a short time

  • Our service providers may keep backups for a limited period before they are overwritten.
  • The other person in a one-to-one chat will see that a chat with a "Deleted account" existed, but none of your information.
