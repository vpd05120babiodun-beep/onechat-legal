OneChat

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 October 2026

1. Who we are

OneChat is a private chat app for families and friends. This policy explains what information the app collects, how it is used, and the choices you have.

If you have any question about your privacy, contact us at onechat.support@gmail.com.

2. What we collect

Account information

  • Your full name, email address, username and phone number. Your phone number is also stored in a list of used numbers so that each number can only be registered once.
  • A profile photo, if you add one.
  • Your password. It is handled by Firebase Authentication and we cannot read it.

What you share in the app

  • Messages, photos, documents, voice notes and locations that you send.
  • Statuses (a photo with an optional caption) that you post.
  • Your connection requests, the people you are connected to, the groups you create or join, and the people you block.
  • The list of people you choose as your emergency contacts.
  • Messages you post in public Rooms, and reports you send about other people's messages.

Technical information

  • A notification token for your phone, and your notification settings.
  • Basic technical data, such as your IP address and device type, which our service providers receive when the app connects to them.
  • Your country, worked out from your internet connection (IP address), so we can place you in your country's Near you room. We keep the country code, not your exact location.

Location

We receive your location in two cases. When you share a location in a chat, it is sent once. When you start an SOS emergency alert, your phone shares your live location with the emergency contacts you chose until you stop, and it stops by itself after 2 hours. This continues while the app is in the background. The app does not track your location at any other time.

3. What we do not collect

  • We do not read the contacts on your phone.
  • We do not use advertising or tracking tools, and we do not show ads.
  • We do not sell your information.

4. How we use your information

  • To create your account and show your profile to the people you are connected to.
  • To deliver messages, files, statuses and notifications.
  • To keep the app safe and prevent misuse.
  • To answer your questions and to meet legal obligations.

5. Who can see your information

  • The people you chat with can see your name, profile photo and what you send to them. Members of a group can see your name, photo and messages in that group.
  • Your username is shown to people you are connected to one-to-one, and to people you send a request to or receive one from.
  • Your email address and phone number are private to you and are not shown to other users.
  • During an SOS alert, only the emergency contacts you chose can see your live location and your name. They are told when you add them.
  • In Rooms, everyone in the room can see your first name, a random number that is different in each room, and your messages. Your username, email address and phone number are never shown there.

6. Service providers

We use these companies to run OneChat. They process information for us:

  • Google Firebase: sign-in, the database that stores accounts and chats, and push notifications.
  • Supabase: storage for photos, documents, voice notes, status photos and profile photos, and the server that sends notifications.
  • OpenStreetMap: shows maps when you open a shared location. OpenStreetMap receives your device's IP address when map images are loaded.
  • GeoJS: only if needed, turns your IP address into a country for the Near you room.
  • Google Fonts: provides the typefaces used in the app.

Your information may be stored on servers outside your country, including in the European Union and the United States.

7. How long we keep it

  • Messages and shared files: removed after 7 days. Expired messages are hidden straight away and deleted from our database the next time the app cleans up, which usually happens when someone opens the chat.
  • Statuses: hidden after 24 hours and deleted when the app next cleans up.
  • Room messages: removed after 3 days. Reports about messages are kept for as long as needed to keep Rooms safe.
  • SOS alerts: your location is updated as you move and is removed within 24 hours after the alert ends, the next time the app cleans up.
  • Profile information and profile photo: kept until you delete your account.
  • Notification token: removed when you log out or delete your account.
  • Our providers may keep short-term backups for a limited time.

8. Your choices and rights

  • Change your profile photo, mute notifications, and block people in the app.
  • Delete your account at any time in Settings > Security & Privacy > Delete account. This removes your profile, username, phone number, photos, statuses and messages. The other person in a one-to-one chat will see "Deleted account".
  • You can also ask us to access, correct or delete your information by email at onechat.support@gmail.com. We will reply within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live, laws such as the Nigeria Data Protection Act 2023 give you rights over your personal data, including access, correction and deletion.

9. Security

Information is sent over encrypted connections and protected by access rules so that only the people in a chat can read it. Messages are not end-to-end encrypted, which means they are stored on our providers' servers until they expire. No system is perfectly secure, so please do not share information you would not want others to see.

10. Children

OneChat is not meant for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

11. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top and, for important changes, tell you in the app.

12. Contact us

Questions or requests about your privacy: onechat.support@gmail.com
